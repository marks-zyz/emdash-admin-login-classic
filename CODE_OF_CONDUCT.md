# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a place where people of every background and level of experience can help without being treated badly. We pledge to make participation free of harassment, whatever someone's age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual identity.

## Standards

Behavior that helps:

- being kind and patient with questions, including ones that have been answered before
- giving feedback on the work, not the person, and taking feedback the same way
- owning mistakes and learning from them
- saying so when you used AI to write something

Behavior that is not acceptable:

- insults, personal attacks, trolling, or sexualized language and attention
- public or private harassment
- publishing someone's private information without permission
- anything else that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and contributions that break this code, and may ban people who repeat or seriously break it. They will say why.

This applies in the repository, its issues, discussions and pull requests, and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems privately through the repository's Security tab ("Report a vulnerability") and say that it is a conduct report. Reports are read only by the maintainers and handled with discretion.
